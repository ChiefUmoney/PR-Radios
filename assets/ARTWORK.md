## Mobile prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Create a production UI skin bitmap based extremely closely on the supplied mobile two-way radio reference. This is a physical radio housing asset for an actual interactive Windows overlay, NOT a screenshot of software. Reproduce realistic black injection-molded plastic, fine pebbled rubber, the ribbed metal top heatsink, silver-edged rotary knobs, small embossed buttons, microphone socket on lower left, orange button on upper far right, directional pad on right, five rectangular soft keys directly below the screen, real recessed seams, subtle wear and studio highlights. Match front-on face, slightly visible upper panel, wide low silhouette around 2.8:1. No stylization, no illustration, no flat vector shapes. Truly photoreal macro product photography. Entire device in frame with 3% margin. TRANSPARENT BACKGROUND with real alpha outside the silhouette; no background plane, no checkerboard, no cast shadow outside radio. High resolution landscape. LCD opening must be perfectly front-facing axis-aligned rectangle, pale gray blank flat screen with NO screen text, NO icons, NO reflections across the screen; all live screen content will be added by app. Keep bezel shading around screen. Remove Motorola branding, put only small clean white uppercase 'PR RADIOS' centered on bezel above LCD. All five softkeys have a small white horizontal dash rather than text. No extra radio, no hand, no additional objects. Reference image is shape/material/layout guide, not an exact source-resolution constraint.
